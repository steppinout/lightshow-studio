# Sequence audit summary

## Aggregate findings

- 9 sequences analyzed
- 43,857 effects identified
- 45 unique effect names observed
- top effects include: On, Shockwave, Off, Color Wash, SingleStrand, Lightning, Ripple, Pinwheel, Curtain, Faces

## Product insight

Simple state, color, and accent effects dominate real home-grown sequences. This means the app must prioritize basic visual sequencing speed and ease of use.

Users are most likely to build satisfying residential shows with a limited set of highly usable effects that can be applied quickly and adjusted without deep low-level control.

## Implication for product design

The product should emphasize:

- straightforward prop and group sequencing
- simple color and state tools
- fast editing of common effect patterns
- strong visual preview of the final show
- user-friendly AI suggestions based on musical mood and energy

This is a product direction note based on aggregate research only, not a copy of any original sequence project or archive.
